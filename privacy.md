# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 31, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how **Football Cognitive Trainer** ("the App", "we", "us") collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you use our mobile application.

## 1. Who We Are

The App is operated by **Sevak Sargsyan Harutyun**, an individual based in Armenia.

Contact for privacy questions: **sevaksargsyan99@gmail.com**

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Account Information
When you register, we collect:
- Email address
- Password (stored in encrypted form by our authentication provider, Supabase; we never see or store it in plain text)
- Display name / nickname
- Role (Coach or Player)
- Organization / team affiliation
- A flag indicating whether the account belongs to a person under 18 ("minor flag"), which you or your academy provide at registration

### 2.2 Profile Information
- Optional profile picture (avatar) that you upload. Accounts identified as belonging to users under 18 cannot upload their own photo
- Likes you receive from teammates

### 2.3 Training and Performance Data
- Decisions made in training scenarios
- Reaction times, scores, correct/incorrect decision counts
- Training history, streaks, remaining "hearts"
- Custom training scenarios created by coaches
- A local offline cache of training sessions stored on your device, synchronized when you are online

### 2.4 Subscription and Payment Information
Subscriptions are processed through **Google Play Billing**. We do not collect or store your payment card details; Google handles them. We receive and store:
- Subscription status (active, canceled, expired)
- Subscription plan type
- Purchase tokens, used only to verify your subscription with Google

### 2.5 Push Notifications
We use **Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM)** to send notifications. This involves storing a device push token associated with your account.

### 2.6 Advertising Data
The free plan shows advertising provided by **Google AdMob**. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the Advertising ID), IP address, and ad interaction data, subject to Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

See Section 5 for how advertising is handled for minors.

### 2.7 Crash and Diagnostic Data
We use **Firebase Crashlytics** to receive crash reports. These may include device model, operating system version, app version, crash logs, and installation identifiers. We use them only to find and fix bugs.

## 3. How We Use Information

We use information to:
- Provide the App's core features (training scenarios, progress tracking, team management, leaderboards)
- Process and verify subscriptions
- Send notifications
- Fix bugs and improve stability
- Show advertising on the free plan
- Protect the App against abuse and comply with legal obligations

We do **not** sell your personal information.

## 5. Advertising and Minors

- Accounts identified as belonging to users under 18 do **not** receive personalized advertising. For these accounts, ad requests are configured as child-directed and under-age-of-consent, and the maximum ad content rating is restricted.
- This protection depends on the minor flag being set correctly at registration. Parents, guardians, and academy administrators are responsible for selecting it accurately.
- Paid subscribers see no advertising.

## 6. Children's Privacy

- **The App is not intended for children under 13.** We do not knowingly allow accounts for children under 13. If we learn that such an account exists, we will delete it and its data.
- Users aged 13 to 17 may use the App only with the consent of a parent or legal guardian, normally as members of a sports academy or club that uses the App.
- By enrolling a minor athlete, the academy or coach confirms that appropriate parental or guardian consent has been obtained for the collection and use of the minor's information as described in this policy.
- We collect only the information needed to provide the App's training features.
- Parents or guardians may review, correct, or request deletion of their child's information by contacting us at **sevaksargsyan99@gmail.com**.

## 7. Who Can See Your Information

- **Coaches** of your team can see your name, training results, and progress.
- **Teammates in your organization** can see your nickname, avatar, scores, and ranking on leaderboards, and can give you likes.
- The App contains no public chat or free-text messaging between users.
- Information is not shown to users outside your organization, except as described in Section 4.
- If you leave a team (or your coach disbands it), your past results are detached from that team and are no longer visible to its coach.

## 8. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law (including the law of the Republic of Armenia on the protection of personal data), you may:
- Request access to the personal data we hold about you
- Request correction of inaccurate data
- Request deletion of your account and data
- Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent

To exercise these rights, email **sevaksargsyan99@gmail.com**.

## 9. How to Delete Your Account

**In the App:** open **Settings → Delete account**, type DELETE to confirm, and your account and associated personal data are deleted immediately. Custom scenarios created by a coach may remain in the App in anonymised form (no longer linked to the coach).

**By email:** if you cannot access the App, send an email to **sevaksargsyan99@gmail.com** from the address registered to your account, with the subject **"Delete my account"**. We will delete your account and associated personal data within **30 days**.

Records we must keep by law (for example, billing records) may be retained. Deleting the App from your device does not delete your account. Active subscriptions must be canceled separately in Google Play.

## 10. Data Retention

We keep your information while your account is active. When you delete your account in the App, personal data is deleted immediately; for email requests, within 30 days. Crash reports are retained according to Firebase's standard retention periods.

## 11. Data Security

We use encrypted connections and the secure authentication practices provided by Supabase. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Material changes will be announced in the App or by email, and the "Last updated" date above will change.
